How it works

You pick the date and the place. We bring the whole simulator, set it up, run it, and take it away. There is nothing for you to build, wire, or configure.

  1. Check your date

    Pick your date and start time on the booking page. We run one booth, so only one event can be on the calendar at a time.

  2. Tell us about the space

    We need a 16 × 20 ft footprint, about 11 ft 6 in of ceiling clearance, one standard outlet, and a level surface. The booking form walks through it.

  3. Reserve with crypto

    A deposit holds your slot. Pay the balance any time before the event. We accept several major coins.

  4. We show up and run it

    We arrive ahead of your start time, build the bay, calibrate the launch monitor, and stay hands-on so your guests just play.

Ceiling height matters. The booth is 11 ft tall. Most residential ceilings and several event-hall door frames are lower than that. Measure before you book — it is the one thing that stops a setup on the day.

This is a premium rig, not a party rental

Every piece of this setup was bought at the top of its category. The tracker is a commercial teaching system. The projector was the best one available. The booth is genuinely massive. That is the difference your guests notice in the first ten seconds.

Uneekor EYE XO launch monitor

Commercial-grade tracking

An overhead dual high-speed camera system — the same class of equipment used in professional teaching studios and fitting bays, not a $200 sensor on a tripod. It captures 24 data points of ball and club data: ball speed, launch angle, backspin, sidespin, club path, face angle, and more. Every shot is measured, not guessed.

Eiki C602S laser projector

6,000 lumens · laser

A commercial short-throw laser projector, not a home-theatre toy. 6,000 lumens of brightness and a 5,000,000:1 contrast ratio mean the course image fills the entire screen at full size and stays bright and readable from the back of the bay — even with the room lights on. Native WUXGA 1920 × 1200 resolution, and a solid-state laser light source rated for 20,000 hours, so it never dims on you mid-event the way a bulb projector does.

Eleven feet of ceiling, twenty feet of depth, your own bay. Because it is oversized, full driver swings are never cramped and guests can stand back and watch. This is a room, not a corner — and it is the reason a company party works in it.

Questions people actually ask

How much space do I really need?

16 ft × 20 ft of clear floor, plus a walkable path to get the booth there. Ceiling clearance of 11 ft 6 in. The booth itself is 11 ft tall, so a standard 8 or 9 ft ceiling will not work.

Does it have to be indoors?

No. Indoors is easiest, but a garage, a covered patio, or a sturdy event tent works as long as the surface is flat, the ceiling height is there, and there is power. Outdoors in wind or rain is not safe for the projection setup.
